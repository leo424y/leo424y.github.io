# Sociology_of_the_Paranormal

Obsidian-compatible Markdown knowledge book on the sociology of paranormal beliefs, experiences, rituals, and knowledge boundaries.

Start here: `Sociology_of_the_Paranormal.md`

Folders:
- `data/` Literature notes with source links
- `note/` Atomic permanent notes and syntheses
- `idea/` Research ideas and actions
- `term/` Concept definitions and links
- `moc/` Maps of content

Internal links use Obsidian wikilinks. Keep filenames unchanged when importing.
