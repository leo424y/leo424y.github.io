# 來源索引與延伸書目

## 哲學百科與學術概述
- [Epistemology in Classical Indian Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-india/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy；印度知識論與 pramāṇa。
- [Śaṅkara](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/shankara/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy；不二吠檀多。
- [Mind in Indian Buddhist Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mind-indian-buddhism/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy；佛教心識與量論。
- [Epistemology in Chinese Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2025/entries/chinese-epistemology/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy；2025 修訂。
- [Episteme and Techne](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/episteme-techne/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy；2024 修訂。
- [Arabic and Islamic Psychology and Philosophy of Mind](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arabic-islamic-mind/) — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy。
- [Jaina philosophy: Epistemology and logic](https://www.rep.routledge.com/articles/overview/jaina-philosophy/v-1/sections/epistemology-and-logic-1) — Routledge Encyclopedia of Philosophy。

## 原典、詞典與傳統文獻
- [Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad](https://texts.wara.in/upanishads/mukhya/mundaka/) — 梵文與英譯，特別是 1.1.4–5。
- [Śaṅkara's Commentary on the Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad](https://indike.org/pramana/in.veda.upanisad.mukhya.mundaka%21sankara~en-sitaramasastri) — 英譯與註釋。
- [Vedic Heritage Portal](https://vedicheritage.gov.in/introduction/) — Veda 詞源與口傳傳統介紹。
- [Monier-Williams: Vidyā](https://kosha.sanskrit.today/word/sa/%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE) — 梵英詞典條目。
- [Outlines of Jaina Philosophy](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/2880.pdf) — IGNCA 開放 PDF；耆那教知識分類與多面性論。

## 論文與研究
- Richard Payne (2010), [“The Authority of the Buddha: The Limits of Knowledge in Medieval Indian Buddhist Epistemology”](https://doi.org/10.15388/AOV.2010.1.3660), *Acta Orientalia Vilnensia* 11(1): 13–36。
- Dustin D. Klinger (2024), [“All knowledge is either conception or assent”](https://doi.org/10.1080/09608788.2024.2400475), *British Journal for the History of Philosophy*；伊斯蘭哲學的概念與判斷區分。
- [“Knowing more by knowing less”](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/735921) — 藏傳佛教社群中的無知概念與民族誌研究。
- [Ancient Indian Concept and Nature of Knowledge](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/8271/1/Unit-6.pdf) — IGNOU 開放教學材料，含 Mīmāṃsā 知識自證理論；屬教學材料，宜與專門研究互證。

## 推薦書籍（後續深讀）
- Roy W. Perrett, *An Introduction to Indian Philosophy*, Cambridge University Press — 印度哲學系統入門。
- B. K. Matilal, *Perception: An Essay on Classical Indian Theories of Knowledge*, Oxford University Press — 印度感知論與知識論的重要研究。
- B. K. Matilal, *The Character of Logic in India* — 印度邏輯與哲學論證。
- Karl H. Potter (ed.), *Encyclopedia of Indian Philosophies* — 各學派原典與研究的參考工具書。
- S. N. Dasgupta, *A History of Indian Philosophy* — 印度哲學史經典綜述；閱讀時需留意其年代與學術史脈絡。
- Jonardon Ganeri, *The Lost Age of Reason: Philosophy in Early Modern India 1450–1700* — 早期現代印度哲學與推理傳統。
- Padmanabh S. Jaini, *The Jaina Path of Purification* — 耆那教教義、修行與解脫論。
- Mark Siderits, *Buddhism as Philosophy: An Introduction* — 佛教哲學入門。
- Richard King, *Early Advaita Vedānta and Buddhism: The Mahāyāna Context of the Gauḍapādīya-kārikā* — 不二吠檀多與佛教思想的歷史互動。
- Seyyed Hossein Nasr, *Knowledge and the Sacred* — 當代傳統主義者對知識與神聖性的論述；應與其他伊斯蘭哲學觀點對讀。
