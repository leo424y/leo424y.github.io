An imagined community is a socially constructed community whose members experience a sense of shared belonging despite not knowing most other members personally. The idea helps explain why a shared working language does not automatically create a shared identity. See [[想像共同體]] and [[區域通用語能促進功能合作卻不必然創造共同身份]].
