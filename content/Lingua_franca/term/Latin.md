Latin is an Indo-European language of the Italic branch. After ceasing to be the everyday native language of most people in Western Europe, it continued as a transregional language of church administration, scholarship, education and learned communication. See [[拉丁語曾支撐中世紀歐洲學術跨地域交流]] and [[拉丁語]].
