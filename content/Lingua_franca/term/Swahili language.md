Swahili (Kiswahili) is a Bantu language with a long history along the East African coast and across Indian Ocean networks. It has served as a regional language of wider communication while remaining rooted in African linguistic histories. See [[斯瓦希里語的區域通用功能根植於東非沿岸與印度洋互動]] and [[斯瓦希里語]].
