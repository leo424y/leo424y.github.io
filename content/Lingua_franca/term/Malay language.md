Malay is an Austronesian language historically used across maritime Southeast Asia. Its use as a trade and interregional language was supported by port cities, shipping, and commercial exchange. See [[15至17世紀馬來語因港口網絡成為東南亞貿易通用語]] and [[馬來語]].
